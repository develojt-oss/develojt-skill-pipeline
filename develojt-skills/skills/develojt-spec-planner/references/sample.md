## Sample commentary

1. **All five blocks are filled** — `[Foundation]` deterministically from the rule table, `[Scene]`–`[Quality]` diverged from the user's free-form text. 4–6 per view, evenly, without bias.

2. **Areas the user struggles to imagine are covered** — `[Edge]` (connection drops, send failures, bursts, storage) is what people rarely think of, and AI filled it with hints. This is the answer to "people can't start from nothing".

3. **No implementation tech** — words like RTSP transcoding, WebRTC, or queueing never appear in the epics. Only intent, like "short enough to be barely noticeable". Quantification and tech decisions are the deriver's.

4. **Business epics belong to the human** — even when an AI hint (#14) and a human epic (#1) look alike, both stay. The human's own sentence ("up to 16") is more concrete, and the deriver reads both together and integrates them.

5. **Volume** — kept at a level a human can skim in 5 minutes.
